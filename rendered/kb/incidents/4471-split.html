The three problems

  1. parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. Tenants who write the day first get flipped dates. This is the bug the merged ticket describes.
  2. hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. People at day-first tenants type what it says, and the form reads it their way. It shows up only on the form channel. Intake often tags these parser, because the symptom looks the same.
  3. one-day shift. The date is off by one day, not swapped (Tuesday shown as Monday; two clients in different cities got different days for one meeting). Time zones are the likely cause. That is a guess, not confirmed.

Why they were merged

The dedupe rule matches on subject line (decision 0001). “Wrong day on the invite” is true of all three problems, so they landed in one ticket. The merge is the cheap choice during an incident. This page is the later split it allows.
